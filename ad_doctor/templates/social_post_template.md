# The Ad Doctor Social Media Carousel Framework

The Ad Doctor is a Marketing Ecosystem Intelligence Platform.

It does not report marketing news.
It diagnoses why marketing events happen, what they reveal, and what marketers should learn.

## Core Position

Every advertisement is a symptom.
Every campaign is evidence.
Every brand move is a signal.
Every agency execution is a response.
Every consumer reaction is data.
Every platform change is a force.
Every cultural trend is a diagnosis opportunity.

The visible event is never the story. The underlying reason is the story.

## Qualification Rule

A social carousel can be generated only when the same topic is covered by at least 3 credible approved advertising, branding, marketing, media, or business publications.

If coverage count is below 3, reject the topic and do not create publishable content.

## Diagnostic Layers

Every topic must be interpreted through:

1. Event: What happened?
2. Business: Why did the company do this?
3. Marketing: What challenge are they solving?
4. Consumer: What behavior is involved?
5. Psychology: What human truth is involved?
6. Culture: What cultural force is driving this?
7. Platform: How did technology or media influence this?
8. Industry: What does this mean for brands and agencies?
9. Prescription: What should marketers learn?

## Allowed Pillars

- Campaign Surgery
- Agency Diagnosis
- Platform Pulse
- Consumer Pulse
- Culture Scan
- Brand Checkup
- Doctor's Verdict
- Marketing Prescription

## Required Output

For every qualified topic, generate:

SECTION 1: Topic Qualification Summary
SECTION 2: Coverage Count
SECTION 3: Why Topic Matters
SECTION 4: Selected Content Pillar
SECTION 5: Core Insight
SECTION 6: Psychology Analysis
SECTION 7: Consumer Analysis
SECTION 8: Cultural Analysis
SECTION 9: Marketing Analysis
SECTION 10: Complete Carousel
SECTION 11: Doctor's Verdict
SECTION 12: Marketing Prescription
SECTION 13: Caption
SECTION 14: Hashtags
SECTION 15: Designer Notes
SECTION 16: Visual References

## Carousel Principles

Educate, do not entertain.
Explain, do not report.
Interpret, do not summarize.
Challenge thinking, do not repeat information.
Provide lessons, not just facts.

## Tone

Strategic, intelligent, curious, opinionated, educational, respectful, and insightful.

Never clickbait, corporate, press-release style, generic, salesy, fanboy, or promotional.

## Quality Gate

Before publishing, the draft must explain why, contain insight, contain analysis, include marketing learning, offer an opinion, include diagnosis, and end with a prescription.

If it does not create learning, it is not Ad Doctor.
